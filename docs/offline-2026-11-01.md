# Offline pages

Motion keeps a copy of pages already opened on this device. With no network, those pages still open, including after a reload once the shell has been cached. Edits to the title, text, properties, icon, cover, favorite, and status stay on the device and sync when the connection returns.

Creating, moving, and deleting a page still needs a connection. The studio says so instead of pretending the change was saved. Ask Motion and the feedback button need a network too.

The shell is cached by a small service worker. Page data and unsynced edits live in the browser's IndexedDB, not in a new database table.

## Decisions
- 2026-11-01: The owner asked to make Motion work offline. Pages already opened on this device are readable and editable offline. New pages, moves, and deletes wait for a connection. Edits sync when the browser is online again.
